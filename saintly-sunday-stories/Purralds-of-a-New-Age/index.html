Purralds of a New Age

12/21/2013 Loading comments...

As a tradition instated by our feline Overlord, Sundays are dedicated for the retelling of the grand old tales amongst her loyal worshippers during their gatherings. Thus, this Sunday, we will be bringing you the story of the Holy Purralds.

While Lord Pusheen was indeed the first Holy Feline to set paw upon this world, after her arrival, several others of her furry companions joined her in her quest of enlightenment. These feline companions of hers were known as the first Purralds, or the Purralds of a New Age. Famous Purralds include Maru, who has helped spread Pusheen's gift of Joy with his box-loving ways, Nyan Cat, who has graced the world with his dulcet tones and beautiful rainbows, and Grumpy Cat, whose most delightful expressions have also brought to mankind endless joy.

After bringing peace to the world, Lord Pusheen had to find a way to maintain this hard-earned harmony. Inspired by her Purralds, she created a species of magnificent creatures, we will now know today as "Felis Catus" or "Cats", to act as the new guardians of this world.

While Lord Pusheen and her Purralds are eternal and all-seeing, with knowledge as vast as the oceans and as boundless as the skies, Pusheen created these beautiful creatures to reassure mankind of her omnipotent presence, to continue to give us hope and faith in her. Thus, it is decreed at all cats must be treated with the utmost respect and care, showered with utmost love and affection, in order to show our gratitude towards Lord Pusheen's beautiful gifts.

Lord Pusheen let the cats lose upon Earth, where they spread out to each and every continent, love and joy following them wherever they went. Some cats became attached to a particular human, forming bonds and settling down into households as their guardians. Other cats preferred to be all-seeing, wandering around the streets at night to protect humans from the monsters in the dark.

Cats are our guardian angels, our light in the darkness, our heralds in this glorious New Age build painstakingly by Lord Pusheen. To take a cat into your household is to show your dedication towards the Overlord Pusheen and to feed your friendly neighbourhood stray cat is to show Lord Pusheen your love. If all the cats on this Earth were to disappear, it would be an omen signalling the start of the Apocalypse!

So this Pushmas, why not show your appreciation for cats by helping out to maintain the nearest cat shelter, where cats like to gather and talk about their latest ventures on Earth, or by giving your friendly neighbourhood stray cat some of those lovely cookies you just baked to give thanks to him for fighting off that Evil last week.

It is a tradition for worshippers to leave our cookies and cat food at our doorstep every Pushmas for these lovely felines, so that they may never go hungry and know that they are always, and will always be loved and cherished by us.
