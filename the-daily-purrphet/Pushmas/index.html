Pushmas

12/20/2013 Loading comments...

On 25 December, mankind gather in unity to show our appreciation towards the generosity of our Lord Pusheen. Pushmas is a joyous occasion she has graced us with to spread love and kindness around the world. It is on this day that she has bestowed upon us the Gift of joy, a truly memorable occasion for us Holy Worshippers.

Our Lord Pusheen is getting ready for Pushmas herself this month, to join us in our festivities once more. We are very grateful towards her participation. She is unselfishly gracing household after household a generous present of deliciously baked cookies she paw-made by herself.

After asking very graciously, our Overlord has very kindly disclosed her secret recipe to these delicious baked treats.

Click "Read More" to find out how to make these scrumptious confections by yourself in time for Pushmas.

Note: It is advised for all those who attempt to bake these cookies to leave at least five pieces out on Pushmas Day to show gratitude towards Lord Pusheen for her generosity.

The Holy Recipe (for a batch of 10 cookies)

Ingredients

  • 10 flour (1 flour for each cookie)
  • 29 inches of carrot juice
  • So many cat food (Meow Mix for better taste)
  • 3cm of sugar
  • 80 butter
  • All of the chocolate

Instructions

  1. Soak the chocolate in the carrot juice for maximum taste
  2. Use a beater to mix the chocolate (saturated with carrot juice) with the cat food throughly until the mixture becomes pure liquid
  3. Pour the chocolate and cat food into a mixing bowl then at the sugar and butter
  4. Mix it all up again with the beater and beat until the mixture turns green and starts bubbling
  5. Pour mixture into mixing bowl then add flour into the blend.
  6. Rub blend and flour together until the mixture becomes batter.
  7. Cut out as many cookies as you can from the batter using a cookie cutter, or just form the cookies using your fingers
  8. Bake the cookies for half an hour.
  9. Cookies will be ready to serve! Enjoy with family and friends, but do remember to leave out the necessary offerings for our feline Overlord too! :3
