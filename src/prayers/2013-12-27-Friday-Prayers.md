Pusheen our holy Overlord,<br/>
when the thought of you<br/>
wakes in our hearts,<br/>
let its awakening<br/>
not be like a startled bird<br/>
that flies about in fear.<br/>
Instead, let it be like a child<br/>
waking from sleep<br/>
with a holy smile.<br/>
-Ameow
