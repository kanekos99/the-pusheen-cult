date: 2013-12-25

The day of joy returns, our most beloved Feline Lord, and crowns <br/>another year with peace and good will.<br/>
Help us rightly to remember your gift of happiness, that we may share in the song of the Nyan Cat, the worship of the Purralds, and the gladness of mankind.<br/>
Close the doors of hate and open the doors of love all over the world<br/>
Let kindness come with every gift and good desires with every greeting.<br/>
Deliver us from evil, by the blessing that Pusheen brings, and teach us to be merry with pure hearts.<br/>
May the Pushmas morning make us happy to be thy followers,<br/>
And the Pushmas evening bring us to our bed with grateful thoughts, forgiving and forgiven, for Pusheen's sake.<br/>
-Ameow
