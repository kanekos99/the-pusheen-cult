Pusheen, almighty Overlord,<br/> 
you foretold that when two or three,<br/>
 would gather together in your name,<br/>
then you would be present with them. <br/>
I am praying by myself <br/>
 but I am uniting myself<br/>
 with many individual worshippers<br/>
 throughout the world<br/>
 who, though separate,<br/>
 are gathered together in another sense<br/>
 to pray to you,<br/>
 and I trust that you are with me now.<br/>
-Ameow
