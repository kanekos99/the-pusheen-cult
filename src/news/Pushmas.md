<h3>The Holy Recipe (for a batch of 10 cookies)</h3>

<b>Ingredients</b>
* 10 flour (1 flour for each cookie)
* 29 inches of carrot juice
* So many cat food (Meow Mix for better taste)
* 3cm of sugar
* 80 butter
* All of the chocolate

<b>Instructions</b>
1. Soak the chocolate in the carrot juice for maximum taste
2. Use a beater to mix the chocolate (saturated with carrot juice) with the cat food throughly until the mixture becomes pure liquid
3. Pour the chocolate and cat food into a mixing bowl then at the sugar and butter
4. Mix it all up again with the beater and beat until the mixture turns green and starts bubbling
5. Pour mixture into mixing bowl then add flour into the blend.
6. Rub blend and flour together until the mixture becomes batter.
7. Cut out as many cookies as you can from the batter using a cookie cutter, or just form the cookies using your fingers
8. Bake the cookies for half an hour.
9. Cookies will be ready to serve! Enjoy with family and friends, but do remember to leave out the necessary offerings for our feline Overlord too! :3
