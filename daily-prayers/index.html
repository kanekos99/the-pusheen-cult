Friday Prayers

12/27/2013 0 Comments

Pusheen our holy Overlord,
when the thought of you
wakes in our hearts,
let its awakening
not be like a startled bird
that flies about in fear.
Instead, let it be like a child
waking from sleep
with a holy smile.
-Ameow

Thursday Devotion

12/26/2013 0 Comments

Pusheen, almighty Overlord,
you foretold that when two or three,
would gather together in your name,
then you would be present with them.
I am praying by myself
but I am uniting myself
with many individual worshippers
throughout the world
who, though separate,
are gathered together in another sense
to pray to you,
and I trust that you are with me now.
-Ameow

Pushmas Day Special

12/25/2013 0 Comments

The day of joy returns, our most beloved Feline Lord, and crowns
another year with peace and good will.
Help us rightly to remember your gift of happiness, that we may share in the song of the Nyan Cat, the worship of the Purralds, and the gladness of mankind.
Close the doors of hate and open the doors of love all over the world
Let kindness come with every gift and good desires with every greeting.
Deliver us from evil, by the blessing that Pusheen brings, and teach us to be merry with pure hearts.
May the Pushmas morning make us happy to be thy followers,
And the Pushmas evening bring us to our bed with grateful thoughts, forgiving and forgiven, for Pusheen's sake.
-Ameow
