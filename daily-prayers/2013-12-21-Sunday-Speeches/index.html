Sunday Speeches

12/21/2013 0 Comments

Holy One of Feline, my comforter hear me o lord and forgive me I ask this of you in your name lord
Our lord my defense and refuge in the day of my trouble I ask you to preserve me we ask this in your name
Lord Pusheen my deliverer I beg thee to forgive me I ask this of you in your name lord
Dear lord in whom I trust we beg of you to forgive my sins I ask this of you in your name lord
My lord the one true God I ask in your name save me from my enemies in your holy name we ask this of thee
Ameow.

Tip: Prayers are best invoked with a generous offering of cookies for the our feline Overlord!
